Welcome to FCA Cash Book

A simple book to write down the money of your cooperative or association:

  • 💰 Money In — sales, membership fees, loans, donations
  • 🛒 Money Out — seeds, fertilizer, wages, transport
  • 💵 Cash on hand — counted for you, always up to date

First, make one book for your group. You can add more books later for other groups you help.
